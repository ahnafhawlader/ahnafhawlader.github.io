The work that excites me

Four sides of materials I want to explore

I like following an idea from what a material is made of to how it is produced, how it performs, and who it helps. These four areas keep me curious.

🦿

Biomaterials

Basketball makes me notice how much movement matters. I am interested in materials that work with the body, especially for medical devices, mobility, and healing.

🏭

Manufacturing with Materials

I enjoy the leap from a sketch to something you can hold and test. Prototyping has made me curious about how material and process choices turn an idea into a consistent, useful product.

🌱

Sustainable Materials Processing

I want to understand how processes use energy and resources, and how better choices can reduce waste while still making materials that perform well.

🔬

Design of Materials, Including Nanomaterials

I love the idea that a small change in structure or surface can change what a material can do. Nanomaterials make me curious about designing properties for applications across health, energy, and beyond.
