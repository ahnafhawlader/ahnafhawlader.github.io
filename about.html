The person behind the projects

More than one thing

I'm Ahnaf. Give me a basketball game, a song that fits the moment, or a big “what if” question, and I can talk for hours. I like the fun of figuring something out, especially with people who see it differently from me.

Being part of the Bengali student community, leading teams, and mentoring younger students have taught me that showing up for people matters as much as having a good idea. I want the spaces I'm part of to feel welcoming and full of possibility.

Engineering is one way I put that curiosity to work. At the University of Toronto, I study Materials Engineering with a Biomaterials theme and a Bioengineering minor. I am interested in how we design materials, make them reliably, and use resources thoughtfully, whether the result supports health, energy, sport, or everyday life.

You'll probably find me...

  • Playing basketball or thinking about the next game.
  • Finding a song to share with someone.
  • Building a Pokémon team just for the fun of it.
  • Helping organize something that brings people together.
  • Asking how an idea could help someone in real life.
